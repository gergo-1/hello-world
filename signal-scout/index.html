How the prediction works

Signal Scout looks for OpenStreetMap features tagged as mobile-phone masts/antennas, then projects a straight line from your current heading and speed. The first projected point outside all estimated mast-radius circles becomes the “edge ahead.” Real service can cut out earlier or later because of terrain, buildings, carrier bands, hand-offs and incomplete tower mapping.

Do not interact with this screen while driving. Set it before moving and rely on audio/notification alerts.
